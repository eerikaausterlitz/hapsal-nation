# Religion of the State of Hapsal

The State of Hapsal recognizes **Sakura Tree Faith** as the primary and traditional religion of the nation.  
The faith is deeply intertwined with Hapsalian culture, identity, and state symbolism, emphasizing harmony, renewal, and duty to the nation.

---

## Sakura Tree Faith

The Sakura Tree Faith centers on the belief that life, governance, and society follow natural cycles, represented by the blooming and falling of sakura blossoms.

### Core Beliefs
- Life is cyclical and must be lived with purpose and discipline.
- Harmony between the state, the people, and nature is essential.
- Loyalty to the nation and respect for tradition are sacred duties.
- Renewal and sacrifice ensure the continuation of society.

---

## Religious Institutions

The faith is organized through a network of **Shrines**, **Custodial Orders**, and **State Ceremonial Offices**.

- **Grand Sakura Shrine** – The highest religious authority, located in the capital.
- **Regional Shrines** – Administer religious affairs within each region.
- **Shrine Custodians** – Priests and caretakers responsible for rites and ceremonies.

---

## Role of Religion in the State

While the State of Hapsal maintains a constitutional monarchy, the Sakura Tree Faith holds a ceremonial and cultural role within governance.

- National ceremonies and state events incorporate religious rites.
- Members of the Royal Family serve as symbolic protectors of the faith.
- Religious traditions influence national holidays and customs.

---

## Religious Freedom

The State of Hapsal permits private worship of other beliefs, provided they do not conflict with public order, national security, or constitutional law.  
The Sakura Tree Faith remains the dominant and state-recognized tradition.

---

## Symbols

- **Sakura Blossom** – Represents renewal, sacrifice, and continuity.
- **Sacred Tree Emblem** – Used in shrines, state ceremonies, and official iconography.
