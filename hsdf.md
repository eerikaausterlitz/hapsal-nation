# Hapsal Self-Defence Forces (HSDF)

The **Hapsal Self-Defence Forces (HSDF)** are the principal defensive forces of the State of Hapsal.  
They are organised into three branches: **Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Force**, **Hapsal Air Self-Defence Force**, and **Hapsal Maritime Self-Defence Force**.

---

## Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Force

The **Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Force** is responsible for protecting Hapsal’s territory and key officials.  
It includes the following units:

### 1. Artic Jaeger Regiment
A specialised unit trained for operations in cold and harsh environments.  
**Primary Duties:** Protect members of the Royal Family, the Prime Minister, and other senior officials.  
[More Info](https://www.roblox.com/communities/1013166238/3-Arctic-Jaeger-Regiment#!/about)

### 2. Infantry Regiment
The principal combat unit of the Ground Self-Defence Force, responsible for territorial defence and conventional military operations.  
[More Info](https://www.roblox.com/communities/1071511656/1-Infantry-Regiment-of-Hapsal#!/about)

**Status:** Active

---

## Hapsal Air Self-Defence Force

The **Hapsal Air Self-Defence Force** is responsible for aerial defence, reconnaissance, and support of the Ground Self-Defence Force.  
**Status:** Closed / Work in Progress

---

## Hapsal Maritime Self-Defence Force

The **Hapsal Maritime Self-Defence Force** oversees naval operations, coastal defence, and support for Ground and Air Self-Defence Forces.  
**Status:** Closed / Work in Progress
