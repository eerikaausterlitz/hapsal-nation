# Hapsal Self-Defence Forces

The **Hapsal Self-Defence Forces (HSDF)** are the principal defensive forces of the State of Hapsal.  
They are organised into three main branches: **Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Forces**, **Hapsal Air Self-Defence Forces**, and **Hapsal Maritime Self-Defence Forces**, each with distinct responsibilities.

---

## Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Forces

The **Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Forces (HGSD)** are responsible for the protection of national territory and key officials.  
The HGSD includes the following units:

### Artic Jaeger Regiment
A specialised unit trained for operations in cold and harsh environments.  
**Primary Duties:** Protect members of the Royal Family, the Prime Minister, and other senior officials.

### Infantry Regiment
The principal combat unit of the Ground Self-Defence Forces, responsible for territorial defence and conventional military operations.

---

## Hapsal Air Self-Defence Forces

The **Hapsal Air Self-Defence Forces (HASDF)** are responsible for aerial defence, reconnaissance, and support of ground operations.  
**Status:** Recruitment and full operations are currently restricted.

---

## Hapsal Maritime Self-Defence Forces

The **Hapsal Maritime Self-Defence Forces (HMSDF)** oversee naval operations, coastal defence, and support for ground and air units.  
**Status:** Recruitment and full operations are currently limited.
