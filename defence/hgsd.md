# Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Forces

The **Hapsal Ground Self-Defence Forces (HGSD)** are responsible for protecting Hapsal’s territory and key officials.  
They are the main land-based component of the Hapsal Self-Defence Forces.

---

## Units

### 1. Artic Jaeger Regiment
A specialised unit trained for operations in cold and harsh environments.  
**Primary Duties:** Protect members of the Royal Family, the Prime Minister, and other senior officials.  
[More Info](https://www.roblox.com/communities/1013166238/3-Arctic-Jaeger-Regiment#!/about)

### 2. Infantry Regiment
The principal combat unit of the Ground Self-Defence Forces, responsible for territorial defence and conventional military operations.  
[More Info](https://www.roblox.com/communities/1071511656/1-Infantry-Regiment-of-Hapsal#!/about)
