# Hapsal State Gendarmerie

The **Hapsal State Gendarmerie** is the principal internal security and law enforcement organisation of the State of Hapsal.  
It assists civilian authorities, maintains public order, and supports national stability.

---

## Units

### Mounted Gendarmerie Battalion
A mobile unit trained for patrol and crowd control duties, providing public safety across urban and rural areas.

### Armoured Response Platoon
An urban-focused emergency and anti-riot unit, equipped with armoured vehicles to respond to high-risk situations.
